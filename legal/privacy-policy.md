# Privacy Policy — Power Prices

**Effective date:** 12 October 2026

Power Prices ("the app") shows Belgian and Dutch electricity prices and can send you price alerts. It is operated by Arnaud Verstraeten ("the developer").

## Summary

- There are no accounts. The app does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
- If you set price alerts, we store your device's push notification token and the thresholds you entered, so we can send you the alerts.
- We do not use ads or analytics, and we do not sell or share your data.

## 1. Data we collect

**Push token and alert thresholds.** When you save price alerts, the app sends us an Expo push token (a random identifier that lets us send notifications to your device) together with the price thresholds you entered. We also store which alerts were last sent, so you are not notified twice for the same event. Nothing else is linked to this token.

**Technical data.** Our hosting provider keeps standard server request logs (such as IP address and timestamps) for security and reliability.

## 2. How we use data

Only to send you the price alerts you asked for.

## 3. Third parties

| Provider | Purpose | Data shared |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Supabase** | Database and server functions | Push token, thresholds, request logs |
| **Expo / Apple Push Notification service** | Delivering notifications | Push token, notification text |

Price data comes from ENTSO-E and Elia. The app sends them no data about you.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Your token and thresholds are kept while you have alerts set. To delete them, clear all alert fields in the app and tap **Save alerts**. They are also removed automatically when the app is uninstalled (we remove tokens that the push service reports as no longer registered).
